Goop Snake

Submission to GMTK Game Jam 2025
Based on the theme Loop.

Links:  Itch page

This is by far my most-played game (perhaps other than Chness back in the day…) because it got featured as “Mark’s Favorite”, the designation that replaced “Winner” as the highest honor for the jam. I get why he changed it but it makes it a lot harder to brag about on a resume!!

Not much else to say about it though, it’s just a solid little puzzle game, and my work on it felt very routine! I think it did so well just because of lucky synergy between a cool idea and the theming of the jam, and of course a lucky couple of days right when the jam landed.

The end ^_^
