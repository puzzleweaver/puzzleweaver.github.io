Fifteen puzzles, my beloved.. But on chness-style grids. I had thought about this idea a lot while working on Chness but had never had the sense to go and do it. As my first project post-chness though, it was perfect!

I took kind of too long on it too, just out of habit and because my brain was kind of still rebooting.. But during this project was definitely when I started to pick up the pieces. It was at the now-defunct (BUT DEARLY MISSED!!!!!!) Cafe&Co in Urbana, Illinois, that I worked on this so much. I remember drinking iced tea during the summer, coming into the cool cafe, and the reddish-brown color of the tea through a tall glass covered in condensation…

It was during this project that I started to decide that it’s best to only program in public :)
