Pollen Pusher

Submission to Ludum Dare 58
Based on the theme Collector.

Links:  Itch page

This one had pretty good results, 26th out of 1361 entries!

I was super happy with the hexagonal grid system and QWEASD controls, I had wanted to do that for a while.

Something notable about the making of this one is, I actually made music for it! And I’m SUPER proud of the vibes that it ended up with, with the wave crashing effects and bee sounds. I did all the sound for this in FL Studio, at Fi’s apartment. She was playing some dark academia music and painting while I worked on it, and I had my headphones on, just on one ear. I usually have a really hard time making music, especially at the ends of jams, and it’s just a sort of fraught thing for me.. But the chill background vibes fixed it and I ended up FLYING through putting it together, staying relaxed, and only taking an hour or two. I’ll definitely be experimenting with similar environments going forwards!!
