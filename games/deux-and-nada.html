Deux and Nada

Submission to Micro Jam 050
Based on themes Aliens and Double or nothing.

Links:  Itch page

Another little blockpusher game, like usual.. This one has two controllable characters: one doubles things she interacts with, the other deletes them. I worked with Nanner and Jamiee on it, and their contributions really made it come to life!!! The story especially is the kind of thing I’m hoping I can elaborate on in the future.. And this game has a lot of room for more puzzles too I think!
