Cult of Done manifesto

I was introduced to the Cult of Done Manifesto by my friend Alice. Thanks Alice! I learned about it while I was working on Fifteent and my Pattern Generator, my first projects post-Chness. I’d like to think I’ve taken it to heart, and looking back I think I’ve worked almost every one of these principles into my mode of operation :v

About the manifesto itself, I absolutely LOVE the ideas it presents. But, I found the original version to be pretty wordy and hard to understand (maybe… they should have edited it a little more 😂). To the point that I had to go through and reword each point myself to really Get all of it! I had also at first separated it out into Facts and Imperatives but retrospectively I think doing so interupts its flow, so I redid it to try to preserve the original’s vibe, but to try to optimize its readability and clarity.

I figure my summarized version might be helpful to someone, so I wanted to include it here 👍

The Cult of Done Manifesto (emma’s summary)

  1. You can only ever be Not Doing, Doing, or Done.
  2. Everything is a draft.
  3. Editing isn’t a thing.
  4. Assume you know what you’re doing, and Do it.
  5. Don’t procrastinate. Abandon ideas after a week.
  6. Be Done so you can move on.
  7. Once you’re Done, throw it away.
  8. Laugh at perfection.
  9. Doing makes you right. Not Doing makes you wrong.
  10. Failure and mistakes count as Done.
  11. Destruction is a kind of Done.
  12. A published idea is a ghost of Done.
  13. Be Done so you can do more.

Done! :3
