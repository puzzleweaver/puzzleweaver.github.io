Cult of Done manifesto

Props to Alice for showing me this. I summarized it because I didn’t like how it was organized.

The Cult of Done Manifesto (emma’s summary)

  1. You can only ever be Not Doing, Doing, or Done.
  2. Everything is a draft.
  3. Editing isn’t a thing.
  4. Assume you know what you’re doing, and Do it.
  5. Don’t procrastinate. Abandon ideas after a week.
  6. Be Done so you can move on.
  7. Once you’re Done, throw it away.
  8. Laugh at perfection.
  9. Doing makes you right. Not Doing makes you wrong.
  10. Failure and mistakes count as Done.
  11. Destruction is a kind of Done.
  12. A published idea is a ghost of Done.
  13. Be Done so you can do more.

Done! :3
