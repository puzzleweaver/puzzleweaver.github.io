Pokemon PNG

Links:  Web

This website only really exists as a companion for my cross-stitch pattern generator. As pokemon sprites are readily available on the internet, this was much easier to make than its sister tool Stardew PNG. So really all I did was download the sprites and pokemon dataset, and make a Vue-based frontend to statically serve them. That’s really it :)

Extra double thanks to eevee for doing the real work of ripping the sprites!!
